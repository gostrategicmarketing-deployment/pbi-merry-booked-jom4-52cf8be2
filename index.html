How these numbers are counted
  • Registrations come from GoHighLevel form submissions on the three paid funnels. A person counts once per step, on the Central day of their first opt-in there. Opt-ins before Nov 10 (team tests) are left out.
  • Lead magnet step 1 is the "Lead Magnet Opt-in Page" (the PDF opt-in). Step 2 is "Challenge Opt-In Page v2", the challenge registration. F1 and F2 count their "Challenge Opt in" step.
  • "Spend into" a funnel is the Meta spend of every ad set whose ads link to it: F1 gets Direct F1 and the F1 VIP Purchase set; F2 gets Direct F2, Warm and the F2 VIP Purchase set.
  • Spend and link clicks come from Meta for the five M&B campaigns in JOM4 (Lead Magnet, Direct F1, Direct F2, Warm, VIP Purchase). The flight ends at noon CT Nov 17.
  • Meta VIPs and ROAS use Meta's pixel Purchase event as Meta attributes it. The lead magnet's VIP Upgrade page still fires $17 instead of $47 (open with the PBI team), so its value reads low there.
  • Hyros numbers are last-click leads and sales per ad, read through the Hyros API at the same time as the rest.
  • GHL's own funnel stats page can count a little differently: it counts submissions, and this page counts people.
