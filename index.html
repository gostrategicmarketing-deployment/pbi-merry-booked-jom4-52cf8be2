          How these numbers are counted
          • Only registrations and VIPs from the ads count. A registration counts when its opt-in link carries the id of an ad in the campaigns below; anyone who came another way (an email, a shared link, a typed address) is left out and only counted in the line under the scoreboard.
          • Registrations are GoHighLevel opt-ins on the three paid funnels, one per person. Lead magnet step 1 is the PDF opt-in; step 2 is the challenge registration. Paid Meta 1 and Paid Meta 2 count their challenge opt-in page.
          • VIPs are paid $17 orders in GoHighLevel, one per person: on the VIP pages of the three paid challenge funnels, and on the standalone VIP page the MOF VIP ads link to ("VIP Standalone FB Ads"). Unpaid orders, the materials fee and the VIP upgrade do not count. A challenge-funnel VIP counts only if their registration came from an ad, and is credited to that ad; a standalone-page VIP is credited to the MOF VIP ads.
          • Spend and link clicks come from Meta for the Merry & Booked campaigns in JOM4 that bring in registrations or VIPs: the five challenge campaigns ($43,100) plus MOF Register Retargeting ($1,500) and MOF VIP ($2,000), and from Nov 12 the two CX Combo Tests campaigns (five ad sets at $200/day each, listed separately as CX-A to CX-E). The MOF FB Group and Day 1 Show-Up campaigns are not included. Cost per registration leaves out the MOF VIP spend (those ads only link to the standalone VIP page, so they cannot bring a registration); it still counts in total spend, budget used and cost per VIP. CTR is link clicks ÷ impressions. Conversion rate is registrations ÷ link clicks.
          • Paid Meta 1 vs Paid Meta 2 compares the cold campaigns that send people to each page (Direct F1 or F2, plus its VIP Purchase ad set, and the cold CX ad sets: CX-B on Paid Meta 1, CX-A, CX-C and CX-D on Paid Meta 2). Warm traffic (Warm and CX-E) only goes to Paid Meta 2, so it is shown on its own.
          • MOF VIP top ads are ranked on VIPs: the buyer's last click to the standalone VIP page names the ad (GHL last-touch link). A buyer whose last click is not the VIP page cannot be traced to an ad and shows only in the totals.
          • Top ads use Hyros last-click leads per ad. For lead magnet ads a Hyros lead is the PDF opt-in.
          • Days are Central time. Today is still running, so its numbers are partial.
