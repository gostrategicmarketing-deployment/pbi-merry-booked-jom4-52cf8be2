        How these numbers are counted
        • Registrations are GoHighLevel opt-ins on the three paid funnels, one per person. Lead magnet step 1 is the PDF opt-in; step 2 is the challenge registration. Paid Meta 1 and Paid Meta 2 count their challenge opt-in page.
        • VIPs are paid $17 orders on the funnels' VIP pages in GoHighLevel, one per person. Unpaid orders, the materials fee and the VIP upgrade do not count. Each VIP is credited to the ad that brought in their registration.
        • Spend and link clicks come from Meta for the five Merry & Booked campaigns in JOM4. CTR is link clicks ÷ impressions. Conversion rate is registrations ÷ link clicks.
        • Paid Meta 1 vs Paid Meta 2 compares the cold campaigns that send people to each page (Direct F1 or F2, plus its VIP Purchase ad set). Warm traffic only goes to Paid Meta 2, so it is shown on its own.
        • Top ads use Hyros last-click leads per ad. For lead magnet ads a Hyros lead is the PDF opt-in.
        • Days are Central time. Today is still running, so its numbers are partial.
